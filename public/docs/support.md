## Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

**How do I create a wallet?**
When you first open Elio Wallet, you'll be guided through creating a new wallet. Your private key is generated securely on your device.

**How do I back up my wallet?**
Go to Settings and select "Export Private Key." Store this key securely offline. Never share it with anyone.

### Security

**Is my wallet secure?**
Yes. Elio Wallet is a self-custodial wallet, meaning only you have access to your private keys. Keys are stored using your device's secure enclave with hardware-level encryption.

**What happens if I lose my device?**
If you've backed up your private key, you can restore your wallet on a new device. Without a backup, your funds cannot be recovered.

### Transactions

**Why is my transaction pending?**
Transactions on Solana are typically confirmed within seconds. If a transaction is pending for longer, it may be due to network congestion. Try again with a higher priority fee.

**What are transaction fees?**
Solana charges small fees for transactions. These fees go to network validators, not to Elio Wallet.

### Troubleshooting

**The app won't open or crashes**
Try these steps:
1. Force close and reopen the app
2. Restart your device
3. Check for app updates
4. Reinstall the app (ensure you have your private key backed up first)

**My balance isn't showing correctly**
Pull down to refresh your balance. If the issue persists, check your network connection and try again.
