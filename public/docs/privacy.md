# Privacy Policy

**Elio Wallet**

**Last Updated: January 17, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Elio Wallet ("we," "us," "our," or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Elio Wallet is a **self-custodial (non-custodial) wallet**, which means you maintain full control over your private keys and funds. We have designed our App with privacy as a core principle.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using Elio Wallet, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information We Do NOT Collect

As a non-custodial wallet, we want to be clear about what we **do not** collect or have access to:

- **Private Keys:** Your private keys are generated and stored locally on your device using secure encryption. We never have access to your private keys.
- **Seed Phrases/Recovery Phrases:** We do not collect, store, or transmit your wallet recovery phrases.
- **Transaction Contents:** We do not collect or store information about your transactions.
- **Account Credentials:** There are no accounts, usernames, passwords, or email addresses associated with your wallet.
- **Personal Identity Information:** We do not require or collect your name, address, government ID, or other personal identification.

### 2.2 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following information is stored **only on your device** and is never transmitted to our servers:

| Data Type | Storage Method | Purpose |
|-----------|----------------|---------|
| Private Key | Secure Enclave (expo-secure-store) | Wallet access and transaction signing |
| Public Wallet Address | Local storage | Display and transaction functionality |
| Biometric Preference | AsyncStorage | User settings |

### 2.3 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the App, certain information may be collected automatically:

#### Device Information

- Device type and model
- Operating system and version
- App version
- Device language settings

#### Usage Information

- App crash reports and error logs (anonymized)
- General app performance data

This information is collected solely to improve app stability and user experience. It does not include any wallet-specific information.

### 2.4 Information from Third-Party Services

To provide functionality, the App interacts with external services:

#### Solana Blockchain Network

- Your **public wallet address** is used to query balances and broadcast transactions
- Transaction data is recorded on the public Solana blockchain
- This is inherent to blockchain technology and is not controlled by us

#### Price and Token Data APIs

- We query third-party APIs to display token prices and metadata, including:
  - **Jupiter** (jup.ag) - for token pricing and swap routing
  - **Helius** (helius.dev) - for RPC services and transaction data
- These queries may include your public wallet address
- We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these services

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the limited information we collect to:

- **Provide the Service:** Enable wallet creation, token management, and transaction functionality
- **Improve the App:** Analyze crash reports and performance data to fix bugs and enhance features
- **Ensure Security:** Monitor for potential security threats or unauthorized use
- **Comply with Law:** Respond to legal requirements when applicable

We do **not** use your information for:

- Advertising or marketing
- Selling to third parties
- Building user profiles
- Tracking across applications

## 4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

### 4.1 We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

### 4.2 Blockchain Transparency

Please be aware that:

- Your **public wallet address** is visible on the Solana blockchain
- All transactions are publicly recorded and viewable
- Anyone can view your wallet balance and transaction history using blockchain explorers
- This is a fundamental aspect of blockchain technology, not a disclosure by us

### 4.3 Limited Disclosure Scenarios

We may share information only in the following circumstances:

| Scenario | What May Be Shared |
|----------|-------------------|

## 5. Data Security

### 5.1 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

- **Secure Storage:** Private keys are stored using your device's secure enclave (expo-secure-store) with hardware-level encryption
- **Local-Only Storage:** Sensitive data never leaves your device
- **Biometric Protection:** Optional Face ID/Touch ID authentication
- **No Central Database:** We do not maintain a database of user wallets or private keys

### 5.2 Your Security Responsibilities

You are responsible for:

- Keeping your device secure with a passcode or biometric lock
- Backing up your private key in a secure, offline location
- Not sharing your private key with anyone
- Keeping your device's operating system and the App updated

### 5.3 Security Limitations

While we implement strong security measures, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of data stored on your device.

## 6. Data Retention

### 6.1 Local Data

Data stored on your device remains there until:

- You delete the App
- You delete your wallet through the App settings
- You manually clear app data

### 6.2 Blockchain Data

Transactions recorded on the Solana blockchain are **permanent and immutable**. This data cannot be deleted by us or anyone else.

### 6.3 Analytics Data

Anonymized analytics and crash data may be retained for up to 24 months to help improve the App.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

### 7.1 Access and Control

You have complete control over your wallet data:

- **Export:** You can export your private key at any time through Settings
- **Delete:** You can delete your wallet and all local data at any time
- **Biometrics:** You can enable or disable biometric authentication

### 7.2 Device Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

| Permission | Purpose | Required |
|------------|---------|----------|
| Camera | Scan QR codes for wallet addresses | Optional |
| Biometrics | Secure authentication | Optional |

You can revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings.

### 7.3 Data Deletion

To delete all your data from the App:

1. Open Settings in the App
2. Select "Delete Wallet"
3. Confirm deletion

**Note:** This will permanently remove your private key from the device. Ensure you have a backup if you wish to access your funds later.
